# Nock Community

## Contributing

Thanks for wanting to contribute! Take a look at the [Contributing Guide](https://github.com/nock/nock/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) for notes on commit message conventions and how to run tests.

Nock has a [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/nock/nock/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). By participating in this project, you agree to abide by its terms.

## Contributors and Sponsors

See the [current list of Nock contributors](https://github.com/nock/nock/graphs/contributors). Contributions of all kinds are welcome.

Support Nock through [Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/nock#sponsor). The [Open Collective page](https://opencollective.com/nock) has current sponsor information.
